🧸 This lock is a toy. To keep the demo simple, the locked message is just a letter you could undo by hand. Real HTTPS uses math so hard that even the world's fastest computers cannot guess it open. Same idea, far stronger.
🔒 You saw both. Over HTTP the snoop read every word. Over HTTPS the snoop got gibberish, but your friend still read it fine. That is what the lock does: it scrambles your data on the way, so people on the same wifi cannot read it.

What the padlock means, and what it does not

The little padlock by a web address is a real, useful sign. But people get fooled because they think it means more than it does. Here is the honest version.

✅ The padlock means: the road is private

It means your data travels in a locked tube. Snoops on the wifi, and others along the way, see only gibberish. The address starts with 🔒 https:// instead of http://.

⚠️ The padlock does NOT mean: this site is honest

A trickster can put a padlock on a fake site just as easily as a real one. The lock only promises the connection is private. It promises nothing about who is on the other end. A scam site with a padlock is still a scam site.

🛡️ The one rule: the padlock keeps your message private on the way there. It does not tell you the person you are talking to is safe. Check the lock, and still check who you trust.
