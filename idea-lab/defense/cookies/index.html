Two kinds of cookies

Not all cookies are the same. Some make your day easier. Some follow you around. Knowing the difference is the whole point.

🤝 Helpful cookies

These help you. They keep you logged in so you do not retype your password, and they remember settings like your theme, language, or what is in your cart. One site, helping one visit flow into the next.

🛰️ Tracking cookies

These help someone else. The same kind of note can be used to follow you across many sites and build a picture of what you look at, often to aim ads at you. Same tool, different job.

✅ The one rule: a cookie is just a note. Some help you, some track you, and you can always delete them. Clearing them logs you out and resets settings, which is the trade.

🧪 See it for real (safe)

In your real browser, open , find the storage or cookies tab, and you can read every note a site left, and delete them yourself. Settings let you block tracking cookies while keeping the helpful ones.
