The check that beats a fake

A message arrives. It sounds exactly right. What do you do?

📞 "It's Grandma. I'm in trouble. Send money right now. Don't tell anyone."
🕵️ You checked another way. Call back on a number you already know. Ask something only the real person would know. A copy cannot pass that, no matter how good it sounds. And remember: rush and secrecy are the scam's favorite tools.

Your superpower

You learned what newsrooms, banks, and security teams actually do. When something matters, they do not trust the message. They verify it another way.

🧠 Carry these three

1. You cannot always spot a fake by looking.
2. You can almost always check another way: a known number, a shared secret, the original source.
3. Urgency is a red flag. Real emergencies survive a one minute check.

✅ The rule: do not trust the message. Trust the check.
