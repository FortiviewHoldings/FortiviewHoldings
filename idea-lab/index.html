/ The wings

Three places to explore

Each wing is a track of small, hands-on labs. Progress and badges are kept on the device, and can be cleared any time.

/ 01

The Foundry

How a rock becomes a computer. Binary, logic gates, adders, memory, pixels, a CPU, and a first program, one rung at a time.

 / 02

Defense Wing

Staying safe online. Strong keys, two-factor, phishing, HTTPS, cookies, and privacy, practiced against pretend targets in a locked sandbox.

 / 03

AI Wing

How AI actually works. Prediction, prompts, hallucinations, bias, and telling real from generated, with a tiny model to train yourself.

Beyond the wings, Mission Control helps a kid turn an idea into something real, and Starcharts tracks progress across everything.
