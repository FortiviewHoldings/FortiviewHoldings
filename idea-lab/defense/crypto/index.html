Why real codes hold

A Caesar cipher fell because it had 25 keys. Real encryption does the same trick, just with a key so enormous that trying them all is hopeless.

Number of keys to try:

Caesar cipher: 25 · a few seconds by hand

Modern key (AES-256):

That is more keys than there are atoms in the whole visible universe.

🌌 Brute force gives up here. Even if every computer on Earth guessed billions of keys a second, working together, they would not finish trying them all before the Sun burns out, or even before the universe gets old. So a strong key cannot be guessed open. It has to be given.
🔑 That is the whole idea: keep the key secret and unpredictable, and make it long enough that the number of guesses is bigger than time itself.
