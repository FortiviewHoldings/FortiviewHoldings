Do you offer hardware that can collect and transmit industrial data securely?

Field Node Technology (OmniBus™)

PragOptics, Bridges Industrial's technology arm, develops open field hardware for industrial environments — buy it assembled, or build it free from the published schematic, BOM, and print-ready case files. Its current device, OmniBus™, is a universal HART communicator featuring:

  • Bluetooth connectivity
  • iOS app integration
  • Standalone field operation
  • Vendor‑agnostic HART communication
  • Calibration Datalogging served over https
  • Local WiFi HotSpot up to 150 Mbps
  • GPS Location Tracking
  • OptiBeacon™ suppot, coming Q4 2028
  • Expected OmniBus™ final release: Q4 2027
