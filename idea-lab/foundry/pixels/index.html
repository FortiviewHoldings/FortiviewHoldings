✦ A picture is a grid of numbers. One dot on or off is a single . Line up enough of them and the bits draw a shape your eyes read as a picture. The computer never sees a smiley. It only sees the 1s and 0s.

Color is numbers too

On and off can only make black and white. To get color, each dot mixes three lights: red, green, and blue. Pick a color, then paint.

Red light: 255 · Green light: 107 · Blue light: 107 This is the color in your brush right now, written as three numbers.

🎨 Each light goes from 0 to 255. 0 means that light is off, 255 means it is all the way up. Mix the three amounts and you get any color: red plus green makes yellow, all three at full makes white, all three at zero makes black. Three numbers, one dot.

💡 A photo is millions of these numbers. Every picture you have ever seen on a screen is a giant grid of pixels, and every pixel is three numbers from 0 to 255. That is the whole secret. Pictures, like everything else in a computer, are just numbers made of .
🔭 A bit of math (the cool part)
🧮 One light from 0 to 255 needs 8 (one byte). Three lights per pixel means 24 bits, or 3 bytes, for one dot. Multiply that by millions of dots and you can see why photos are big files.
