03 PragOptics

Where ideas become real.

PragOptics is the hardware and software group. We build the Omni product line of open field hardware, design custom boards and control logic for one-off projects, and run short production batches in photopolymer resin. On the software side we build custom data connections and serve them securely: take the API into your own system, or have us host it with a front end to work in. Alongside that, a studio for websites and web apps, managed Microsoft 365, live dashboards, and the PragOptics platform, in development, that puts those same tools in your hands. One secure tenant, fully managed, billed by simple per-user tiers.

/ The platform

One stack, hardware to cloud

Hardware that reads the field, connections that carry what it reads, and the managed stack around both.

/ 01

Field Node Manager

The free companion for our hardware. It connects the Omni product line over HART, streams live instrument data, and turns every connection into a certified, audited record.

/ 02

The PragOptics platform

Build and publish web apps and notebooks, clean up and visualize data, and write your own function logic for real hardware: your own FNM, running your logic.

/ 03

3D Suite

Model and slice in one app, with the same print profiles we run in production, to take a part from idea to physical print.

/ 04

Custom connections

Instruments, systems, and data sources connected by logic we write, then served securely. Take the API into your own system, or have us host it with a front end to work in. Hosting comes with the API, it is the same connection either way.

/ 05

Managed Microsoft 365

Branded email under your domain, SharePoint, Teams, and Office, plus Power Automate and PowerApps flows and the mobile apps that follow you into the field. Identity, DNS, licensing, SPF, DKIM, and DMARC handled for you. Cyber-aware by default, not as an upsell.

/ 06

Dashboards & visualization

The front end that comes with a hosted connection: live operational dashboards and analytics that turn field and business data into something you can act on.
