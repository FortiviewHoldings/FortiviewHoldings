/ Hard problems

The measurement that will not behave

An intermittent reading. A loop that drifts on night shift and nowhere else. A level that is right until the tank gets low. A transmitter that has been replaced twice and still lies to you.

These are the calls we take. Most measurement problems are not instrument failures. They are a process meeting an instrument that was never matched to it: product flashing across an orifice, low-density solids that radar sees straight through, coating, foam, entrained gas, stratification, temperature swings the datasheet never mentioned.

We find out which it is before anyone spends money. Often the instrument you already own is the right one, mounted, ranged, or configured wrong. Sometimes the technology genuinely cannot see your product, and we will tell you that too, with the reasoning behind it.

Intermittent and unrepeatable faultsProblems that behave while someone is watching and return when nobody is.
Hard-to-measure productsFlashing and two-phase flow, low-density solids, foam, slurries, coating and buildup, entrained gas.
Process-induced measurement errorWhere the reading is wrong because of what the process is doing, not because the device has failed.
Repeat failures and serial replacementsThe same device dying again and again is a symptom. We look for what keeps killing it.
