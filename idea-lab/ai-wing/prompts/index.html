Talking to AI

A fuzzy ask gets a fuzzy answer. Learn to ask in a way that gets you what you want.

Two ways to ask

Same robot, same moment. One kid is vague. One kid is clear. Look at what each one gets back.

Vague ask "Write something." No topic. No length. No idea who it is for.
Clear ask "Write 3 sentences about sea otters for a 7 year old, in a fun voice." Topic, length, and who it is for. All spelled out.
💬 AI cannot read your mind. It only has the words you give it. The more it has to guess, the more it guesses wrong. Clear words in, useful answer out.

The habit that matters most

A clear ask gets a better answer. But "better" is not the same as "true." So there is one last step, every single time.

🧠 Three things to carry

  • 🎯Be specific. Say the topic, the length, and who it is for. Guessing is the robot's job to avoid, not yours.
  • 🧩Give context. Tell it what you already know and what you want it to do. More clues, fewer wrong turns.
  • 🔍Always check the answer. AI can sound sure and still be wrong. Read it yourself before you trust it or share it.
✅ The rule: ask clearly, then check carefully. A good question is half the work, and your own eyes are the other half.
