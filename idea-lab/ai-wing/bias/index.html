Where AI Learns

An AI only knows what you show it. Feed a sorter examples, then watch what happens when the examples are lopsided.

Meet the sorter

This is a tiny sorter. Its only job is to look at a new picture and say one word: cat or dog. It has never seen a single one yet. You are its teacher.

📚 An AI learns from examples, not rules. Nobody types in "a cat has pointy ears." You show it labeled pictures, and it copies the patterns it sees. That means the examples you pick become the whole world it knows.

Teach it (the easy, lazy way)

The big idea

You just saw the most important rule in all of AI, and you saw it for real, not as a slogan.

🧠 Carry these three

1. An AI only learns what its examples show it.
2. If the examples are lopsided or unfair, the AI will be too. That is called bias, and it is real.
3. Garbage in, garbage out. Fair examples in, fairer results out.

⚖️ This matters in the real world. Real AI sorts job forms, photos, and voices. When its examples leave a group out, it gets that group wrong, the same way your sorter missed dogs. People whose job is to build AI well spend a lot of time checking that the examples are fair.
