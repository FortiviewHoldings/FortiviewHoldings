The wild story

Your friend sends you this. What do you do?

📣 "They are banning homework FOREVER, starting Monday! Pass it on!!"

Trace it back

Follow the story upstream. Who did each person hear it from? Keep tracing.

🕳️ The trail ends in the dark. No school, no news, nobody who would actually know ever said it. A story is only as trustworthy as where it started, and this one started from nowhere.

Who would you trust?

For each one, tap Trust or Be careful. Where would a real answer come from?

🧭 Trust sources that have to get it right and can be checked: an official site, or the same story from two places that do not copy each other. Be careful with reposts, memes, and screenshots you cannot trace.

The habit that beats a rumor

Before you believe it or share it, ask three quick things:
1. Who said it first? Trace it to the original source, not the person who reposted it.
2. Does anyone reliable confirm it? Look for it from a place that has to get it right.
3. When is it from? Old stories get dressed up as new all the time.

✅ The rule: trace before you trust. And never be the one who passes a rumor along.
