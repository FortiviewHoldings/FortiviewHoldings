🔭 A real rhyme (the cool part)
🪐 The gravity that holds planets around the sun and the electric pull inside an atom follow the same math: twice as far away, one quarter the pull. That is why people first drew the atom like a tiny solar system. The real atom turned out fuzzier and stranger, but the guess was a good one.
