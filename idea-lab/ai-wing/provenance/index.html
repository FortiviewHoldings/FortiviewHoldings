Stack the signals

No one check is proof on its own. A real original can still be reused in a lie, and a missing label does not mean fake. Investigators do what you just did: gather several signals and weigh them together.

🔎 The three checks, for any image

1. Reverse image search. Does it show up earlier somewhere else, or in a totally different story? Then this caption is not the real one.
2. Content Credentials (). A tamper-evident label some cameras and AI tools now attach, showing if an image was AI-made or edited. If it is there, read it.
3. Source that has to get it right. Did it come from a place that is accountable, or from an account built for clicks?

✅ The rule: one signal is a hint, several together are an answer. Check the source, not the surface. And when the checks disagree, stay in doubt until they do not.
