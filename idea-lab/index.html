🪐 For grownups and teachers

The Idea Lab is free and asks for nothing. There is no account, no login, no email, and no data about your child leaves this browser. Progress is kept only on this device and can be cleared at any time. Everything is built on facts you can check, and the safety labs run against pretend targets inside a locked sandbox, so a child can never reach a real website. Our goal is simple: turn screen time into building time.
