Frequently asked questions

Custom Website Development

Yes! Bridges Industrial builds modern, responsive websites tailored to your brand, operational needs, or business workflow. We handle everything from domain setup to long‑term hosting and maintenance.

  • Full website design and development
  • Domain registration & DNS management
  • Secure hosting and infrastructure
  • Custom business portals and dashboards
  • Enterprise‑grade systems built for small business budgets
Can you build online stores or POS systems for my business?

eCommerce & Custom POS Systems

Yes! We develop custom eCommerce platforms and point‑of‑sale systems that integrate seamlessly with your business operations. These solutions can be standalone or connected to your inventory, CRM, or financial tools.

  • Online storefronts and product management
  • Retail and restaurant POS systems
  • Order processing, fulfillment, and customer tracking
  • Integration with payment processors and accounting systems
Can you replace expensive or disconnected software?

Custom API Development

Bridges Industrial builds secure, scalable APIs that allow your systems to communicate reliably across platforms. Whether you need a bridge between field devices, cloud services, mobile apps, or internal tools, we design APIs that fit your exact operational requirements, without unecessary overhead.

Can you build dashboards or analytics for my data?

Data Acquisition & System Visibility

We provide complete data acquisition handling and operational visibility solutions. Our systems are designed to bolt onto your existing infrastructure, enabling real‑time insight without requiring a full system replacement.

  • Field data collection and aggregation
  • Live dashboards and operational analytics
  • Remote device monitoring and configuration
  • Integration with existing PLC, DCS, or historian systems
Do you offer hardware that can collect and transmit industrial data securely?

The Omni Product Line

Yes. PragOptics, our hardware and software group, builds the Omni product line of open field hardware. Buy it assembled, or build it free from the published schematic, BOM, and print-ready case files.

  • OmniSource™, a pocket loop-power supply. Released.
  • OmniPulse™, in development, arriving ahead of OmniBus.
  • OmniBus™, a universal, vendor-agnostic HART communicator and calibration recorder. Expected Q4 2027.

OmniBus carries Bluetooth, iOS app integration, standalone field operation, calibration datalogging over HTTPS, a local WiFi hotspot up to 150 Mbps, GPS location tracking, and OptiBeacon™ support from Q4 2028.

Do you support industrial automation or instrumentation in the field?

Industrial Automation & Instrumentation Support

Bridges Industrial provides full‑spectrum industrial automation support and instrumentation technical services across all major brands and platforms.

  • Calibration, configuration, and troubleshooting of industrial instrumentation
  • Pneumatic, digital, and smart devices for flow, level, temperature, and analytical measurement
  • Advanced instrumentation support including Coriolis, magnetic flow, ultrasonic, radar, and guided‑wave radar transmitters across all major brands
  • HART communication, device diagnostics, and smart transmitter support
  • PLC, and DCS troubleshooting, logic development, and system optimization
  • Startup, commissioning, loop verification, and integration across all major OEM platforms

Whether you need digital infrastructure, business systems, field hardware, or industrial automation support, Bridges Industrial provides solutions that scale with your operation.

Can you design and build custom hardware for a project?

Custom Hardware Design & Build

Yes. PragOptics designs and builds custom field-data devices, control boards, and enclosures, from a one-off proof of concept through a short production batch. That covers schematic capture, PCB layout, board bring-up, firmware, enclosure design, and assembly.

The Omni product line is the open-hardware example of that work.

Do you do 3D printing or short-run production parts?

MSLA Resin Printing & Short-Run Production

Yes. We run MSLA (masked stereolithography) printing in photopolymer resin, which suits parts needing fine feature detail and a smooth surface finish: prototypes, custom enclosures, jigs and fixtures, replacement components, and short batch runs.

Can you write custom control logic for a machine or process?

Function & Control Logic

Yes. We develop function and control logic for custom projects, whether you are commissioning something new or replacing logic on equipment already in service. That covers PLC and DCS logic across major OEM platforms, motor, relay and interlock logic, PID tuning, safety instrumented functions, and custom sequence logic for one-off machines.

Can I use your software to build things myself?

The PragOptics Platform

That is the goal. The PragOptics platform, now in development, puts our own workflow in your hands: design your own hardware, write and test your own function logic against real hardware, build and publish web apps, and clean up and visualize your data. Your own Field Node Manager, running your logic instead of ours. It runs in the browser, with a cloud option that provisions environments and storage once an account is active.
