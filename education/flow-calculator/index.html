Equations, standards & implementation coverage
Coverage is explicit, not a blanket compliance claim. Each DP type uses its own equation or a clearly identified entered-coefficient model. Geometry, taps, installation, uncertainty, real-gas properties and equipment ratings remain the user's responsibility. This is not a control-valve Cv/Kv, choked-flow, wet-steam, multiphase, open-channel or pipe-network solver.
S1 · Classical Venturi

ISO 5167-4:2022 numerical coefficient profiles and expansibility; checks D, β, Re and pressure ratio. Physical geometry, roughness and straight lengths are not audited.

ISO 5167-4:2022
S2 · Orifice plate

Reader–Harris/Gallagher (1998) coefficient with corner, flange or D–D/2 taps. Equation transcription and numerical bounds verified against ISO 5167-2:2003 §§5.3.1–5.3.2; the current standard is linked separately. No extrapolation below its Re limit.

ISO equation source (2003) · Current ISO 5167-2:2022 scope
S3 · Nozzles

ISA 1932, long-radius and Venturi-nozzle correlations transcribed from ISO 5167-3:2003; ISA 1932 also cross-checked in the 2020 preview. Throat-tapped mode requires entered C and Y. “ISA 1932” names a geometry, not modern ISA control-valve sizing.

ISO nozzle equation source · ISO 5167-3:2022 scope
S4 · Cone, wedge & custom elements

Entered C/Y or K/Y only; no automatic ISO 5167-5/-6 coefficient claim. β² is the open-area fraction for cone and wedge. A coefficient must match the equation displayed, not just the letter K or C.

ISO 5167-6:2022 scope
S5 · Pitot / averaging pitot

Dimensionless coefficient in Qa = K·Y·A·√(2ΔP/ρ). Single-point mode adds an entered mean/local velocity factor. No manufacturer's proprietary coefficient curve is inferred.

Primary-element theory reference
S6 · Flow units & reference states

SI conversion core; absolute P and T; independent flowing and reference Z. Normal units are fixed at 0 °C / 101.325 kPa abs. Standard units use the selected, printed reference state.

NIST gas-flow reference warning · NIST unit factors
S7 · Signal & continuity models

Q = v̄A; frequency divided by pulse K; affine analog scaling or one square-root extraction. These are transparent algebraic conversions, not a new calibration for magnetic, ultrasonic, vortex, turbine, thermal or Coriolis instruments.

S8 · Curves & limitations

Each plotted point is recalculated. No-data regions are not bridged. Pressure/temperature sweeps hold entered composition, Z, viscosity, κ and calibration constants fixed; they are sensitivity curves, not an EOS or equipment performance prediction.

S9 · Source review

13 October 2026. No copied standards or proprietary calibration databases are bundled. Linked standards remain the authority for their applicable edition. Run the embedded software checks; passing arithmetic tests does not certify a physical installation.
