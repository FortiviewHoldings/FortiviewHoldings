What this means for you

You just saw the engine behind every chatbot. It is a next-word guesser, trained on mountains of text, picking what tends to fit.

🧠 Carry these three

1. AI learned which words tend to follow which, from huge amounts of text.
2. It predicts what fits. It does not understand meaning the way you do.
3. So it can sound sure and still be wrong. Smart answers still need your check.

✦ The rule: good words are not the same as true words. The thinking is still your job.
